# Next wall after v0.7: make credit spatial, not the field more complicated

v0.7 says the extracellular field can function as a proposal/guidance channel. The weak point is now delayed stabilization.

Keep fixed:

- v0.7 quasi-static extracellular solve;
- binary fixed-speed material;
- persistent one-cell tips;
- separate retraction;
- matched phase controls.

Compare three credit carriers under identical growth proposals:

1. **global scalar** — current v0.7 reward applied everywhere through structural eligibility;
2. **retrograde path credit** — soma consequence travels backward along recently active arbor with a finite propagation/decay time;
3. **diffusible trophic credit** — soma emits a slow scalar field after useful consequence; recently eligible growth responds to the product.

Primary test: does a spatial credit carrier beat global/no-credit on held-out temporal discrimination while keeping proposal counts matched?

Only after credit is robust should phase be revisited. Otherwise phase tuning just optimizes proposal statistics around a weak selector.
